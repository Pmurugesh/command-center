Company OS Map

The whole system in one drawing, then the one loop the business runs on, then each agent before and after the redesign. Red dashed means broken or missing today; green means added by the redesign; numbered dots are the gaps, listed once below Figure 1.

Read it as an operating system

KernelThe operations git repo. One truth, every change a commit, synced both ways by the janitor. Nothing else holds a fact for long.
SyscallsThe dashboard API. The only sanctioned write. Agents that wrote around it (crons, files, action items) are being routed through it.
ProcessesSix agents (judgment) and a dozen scripts (deterministic, zero tokens). The redesign moves work from agents to scripts wherever no judgment is needed.
SchedulerOpenClaw cron for agent turns, launchd for daemons. Declared in git, applied on deploy, alerting on failure.
DevicesMailbox, workbench, procurement export, Granola, calendar, public pages, repos. Three of them are blind today; no output device sends mail.
ShellToday and the dashboard pages for reading; Telegram for talking to Paladin. The team has neither.

What the OS still lacks after wave 1

Four things are not code and cannot be merged: a send device (gap 5), the team's Sent folders or a shared address (gap 1), the team's channel (gap 4), and a ruling on whether Infinite Solutions leads belong in the store (gap 9). Everything else in red has a PR or a mini-session step. Two more are worth naming because no diagram shows them: the roadmap and the CRM are still two vocabularies for the same deals (a milestone "reached verbal-commitment" is the contact's stage rendered three times on Today), and the price is the one fact the whole GTM layer keys on and it still lives in a draft.
